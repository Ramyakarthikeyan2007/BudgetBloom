BudgetBloom

Version 1.0.0

BudgetBloom is an elegant, client-side personal finance planner engineered to cultivate mindful financial habits. Designed with a soothing emerald green and warm cream palette, it gives you absolute clarity over your monthly budgets, categorized expenses, and spending trajectory.

Core Principles

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: All data is stored in your browser's LocalStorage. No server, no database, no trackers, and no third-party data collection.
  • Zero-Lag Responsiveness: Built with pure HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript for blazing-fast performance.
  • Strict Per-Month Budgeting: Budget goals and expenses are strictly isolated by month so you can compare progress over time without cross-contamination.
  • Multi-Currency Ready: Full support for Indian Rupee (₹) and US Dollar ($) with consistent locale formatting.
